New to Pickleball?

Tennis, badminton, and ping-pong walk into a court.

Pickleball is played with solid paddles and a perforated plastic ball on a court about a third the size of a tennis court — easy to pick up in minutes, hard to put down after your first rally.

It's social, low-impact, and genuinely competitive at every level — which is exactly why it's become the fastest-growing sport on the planet.
